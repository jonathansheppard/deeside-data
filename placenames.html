The lost names of your street

Before the houses, most of Flintshire was fields, and every field had a name. Enter a postcode to see the old names recorded within about 500 metres.

    Source: List of Historic Place Names of Wales, Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments of Wales, via DataMap Wales, Open Government Licence. Most field names come from the tithe maps of the 1840s. Positions are within about 100 metres. Covers the historic county of Flintshire. Built by Deeside.com.
