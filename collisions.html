Source: Department for Transport road casualty statistics (STATS19), Open Government Licence v3. Only collisions where someone was hurt and police were told are included; crashes with damage only are not. "Serious" covers injuries such as broken bones or a stay in hospital.

Locations: police officers who attend record each collision as a single point on the Ordnance Survey National Grid, usually to within 1 to 10 metres, with a written description. Councils can correct a location using their knowledge of the road, and the Department for Transport carries out quality checks, but some errors may remain.
